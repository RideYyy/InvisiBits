The method at a glance
How it works

        

          
01
Prepare
Text is converted to UTF-8, compressed with Deflate when beneficial, and optionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

          
02
Embed
Every container bit is written into the least significant bit of an RGB channel or PCM sample. Each value changes by no more than one.

          
03
Verify
A signature, lengths, and CRC32 distinguish InvisiBits messages from random data and detect corruption.
